# A Tope Burguer — Product

## What it is
Fast-food burger restaurant in California, USA. Open afternoons and nights. Landing page (single route, `index.html`) whose job is to turn a visit into an order (DoorDash / Uber Eats) or a trip to the store (Google Maps) — both weighted equally.

## Audience
Families, students, workers (incl. night shift) and Hispanic customers in California. Bilingual EN/ES, language toggle always visible.

## Voice
Urban, energetic, direct. Racing + fire humor taken from the logo, never aggressive. Spanish is natural US Spanish, not translated English.

## Truth rules
- No invented reviews, dishes, prices, ratings or claims. Real data lives in `js/config.js`; empty values render honest "coming soon" states.
- Claims pending confirmation: grill cooking, combos, bilingual staff.

## Brand
Logo `img/logo.jpg`: black roundel, red flame, silver bun, two checkered flags, gold star. Palette: black, flame red, silver/chrome, trophy gold (accent only).

## Constraints
Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no frameworks, opens by double-click (file://). WCAG 2.1 AA. Texts in `js/i18n.js`; external links only in `js/config.js`. CCPA/CPRA privacy notice required; cookie notice only if analytics are added.

## Surface: landing (Persuade)
Thesis: "Two lanes, one starting line." Start-light tree shows live open/closed status from configured hours; lanes 1/2 = deliver / come get it; checkered band only at the final CTA.
