Tus derechos en California

Según la CCPA/CPRA puedes pedir saber qué datos tenemos de ti, corregirlos o borrarlos. Escríbenos desde el formulario de contacto y te respondemos.

Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

  • What you type in the contact form: name, email and message.
  • Your language preference, stored only in your browser.

We don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

How we use it

Only to reply to your message. We don't sell or share your personal information.

The Google map only loads if you press “Load map”; at that point Google receives data about your visit. The DoorDash, Uber Eats and social media buttons take you to their sites, which have their own policies.

Your California rights

Under the CCPA/CPRA you can ask to know, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Write to us through the contact form and we'll respond.

Accesibilidad · Accessibility

Queremos que todos puedan pedir. Este sitio busca cumplir las pautas WCAG 2.1 nivel AA: contraste suficiente, navegación con teclado, textos alternativos y formularios con etiquetas claras. Si algo no te funciona, escríbenos desde el formulario de contacto y lo arreglamos.

We want everyone to be able to order. This site aims to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA: sufficient contrast, keyboard navigation, text alternatives and clearly labeled forms. If something doesn't work for you, write to us through the contact form and we'll fix it.
